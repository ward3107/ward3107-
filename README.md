### 🧭 About

I'm a **full-stack developer** and **English teacher / ICT coordinator** (רכז תקשוב) in Israel. My day job in the classroom is where the product ideas come from — I ship tools that solve real problems for real students, in Hebrew, Arabic, and English.

- 🎓 Currently building for the Israeli education system
- 🧠 Deep interest in **RTL UX**, gamified learning, and career-guidance psychology
- 🌍 Trilingual builder: fluent Hebrew · Arabic · English
- 💡 Open to collaborations on edtech, RTL SaaS, and AI-powered learning
